Figure 1: Regression Fit

Regression
Analysis: This scatter plot visualizes the linear relationship between your selected variables. The blue dots represent your actual data, while the dark line represents the fitted regression model.

Look at the R-Squared value: it ranges from 0 to 1. A value close to 1 indicates that the X variable strongly explains the variation in Y (a tight fit). A value close to 0 suggests little to no linear relationship. If the dots form a curve instead of a straight line, a linear model may be insufficient. The residuals (distance of dots from line) show the model error. Consistent patterns in residuals imply bias.
